# 2017-2018-The-Ultimate-Collection-of-Chinese-Reports
> From the perspective of a data analyst and corporate BI engineer, I have compiled data across 21 major categories. These cover the essentials of daily life (food, clothing, housing, and transportation); the four major societal challenges—healthcare, education, child-rearing, and elderly care; the full lifecycle of AI & 3C products (from R&D and manufacturing to sales and after-sales service); as well as the sectors of entertainment, finance, and media.
本人以数据分析师和企业BI工程师角度收集，共计 21 大类，基本涵盖衣食住行、医疗教育育儿养老四大难题、人工智能和 3C 产品生命周期全链路（从研发到制造到销售到售后）、泛娱乐、金融、传媒各方面。

## 一、核心结论

- 中国 SaaS CRM 在 **2015 年进入元年**，2014—2020 年处于高速发展期，**2017—2018 年为关键窗口期**。
- SaaS 市场普及率仍 **不到 10%**，增长空间大。
- 行业趋势从单一 CRM 工具，升级为 **SaaS + PaaS + AppStore 生态模式**。
- 融资环境趋冷，但资金更集中流向头部企业，创新者机会减少。
